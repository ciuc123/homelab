## What to fix first

Ordered by impact: lost visitors and leads first, then how the site shows up in search, social and AI answers, then polish.

1. **Internal links point to pages that exist** — 1 failing. Links to pages that don't exist lose readers and tell search engines the site isn't maintained.  
   **Fix:** Fix each listed link to point at a page that exists, or remove it.  
   Where: `/posts/2025-11-05-laravel-11-slim-architecture/` (broken: /posts/2024-11-08-laravel-11-slim-architecture)
2. **Private repo files aren't published** — 1 failing. Files meant for developers are publicly downloadable. They can expose internals and they waste crawl budget.  
   **Fix:** Exclude them from the build or block them at the server so they return 404.  
   Where: `site` (reachable: /README.md)
3. **Page has og:image** — 29 failing. Links shared on LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp or X show no picture and get far fewer clicks.  
   **Fix:** Add an og:image (1200×630) per page, or at least a branded site-wide default.  
   Where: `/posts/2024-06-10-hello-world/` (no og:image); `/posts/2025-08-09-remove-secret-content/` (no og:image); `/posts/2025-09-12-php-security-audit-lessons-laravel-developers/` (no og:image); and 26 more
4. **Page has exactly one `<h1>`** — 7 failing. The `<h1>` is the page's main heading. None, or several, blurs what the page is about.  
   **Fix:** Use exactly one `<h1>` per page, usually the title, and `<h2>`/`<h3>` for sections.  
   Where: `/posts/2025-09-15-phpstorm-laravel-xdebug/` (2 `<h1>` elements); `/posts/2025-09-17-php-84-performance-boost/` (2 `<h1>` elements); `/posts/2025-09-23-laravel-security-crisis-app-key-rce/` (2 `<h1>` elements); and 4 more
5. **llms.txt exists and isn't empty** — 1 failing. llms.txt gives AI assistants a short, curated summary of who you are and your best pages.  
   **Fix:** Publish /llms.txt: one paragraph about the business, the services, how to get in touch, and links to the key pages.  
   Where: `/llms.txt` (HTTP 404)
6. **Every sitemap page is linked from another page** — 1 failing. Pages no other page links to are hard for both visitors and crawlers to find, and rank poorly.  
   **Fix:** Link to each listed page from at least one related page or a menu.  
   Where: `/AGENT_PROMPTS/` (no other page links here)
7. **Meta description is 50-160 characters** — 17 failing. Descriptions that are too long get cut off; too short ones waste the space.  
   **Fix:** Rewrite each listed description to 50–160 characters.  
   Where: `/posts/2024-06-10-hello-world/` (meta description is 36 chars); `/posts/2025-09-12-php-security-audit-lessons-laravel-developers/` (meta description is 316 chars); `/posts/2025-09-14-php-property-hooks-game-changer/` (meta description is 438 chars); and 14 more
